'wavy-physics': minor
---

Turn the package into `wavy-physics`, a wave-only physics library: split-operator Schrödinger solvers (1D, 2D, imaginary time), exact hydrogen waves and Rydberg packets, the radial wave equation, Dirac waves, nonlinear Schrödinger solitons, Maxwell FDTD, Liénard–Wiechert and Larmor radiation, classical and fractal waves, and a 30-benchmark validation suite against NIST and exact results with a `wavy-physics validate` CLI. The example app becomes the Wavy Physics website, which shows an atom waving in time, an electron wave packet orbiting the nucleus, and light radiated by accelerating charges.
